Things to Notice
Velocity Distribution

Notice that the velocity distribution does not depend on g. Any force could act on the particles, but the distribution is always the same.

Particle Coloring

Since the particles are simulated in 3D space, but the screen is just 2D, they are colored based on how far away they are from the camera. The lighter shades are further back.

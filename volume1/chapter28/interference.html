Things to Notice
Destructive Interference

Move $\theta$ to $180°$, and point $D$ to the x-axis. It is nicely visible that the green arrow, the electric field at point $D$, is always zero, because the electric radiation from the two charges cancels out. It is also visible in the whole field, that the waves are the strongest diagonally.

Constructive Interference

Move $\theta$ to $0°$, and point $D$ to the x-axis. It is nicely visible that the green arrow, the electric field at point $D$, oscillates with a large amplitude, even larger, than it would move if there was only one charge. It is also visible in the whole field, that the waves are the strongest on the x-axis. This effect would be even greater if we were to add more charges.

Electric Field Vectors

The opacity of the electric field vectors is dynamically scaled based on their magnitude. This is a visual effect used to make the propagating waves clearly visible across the canvas.
